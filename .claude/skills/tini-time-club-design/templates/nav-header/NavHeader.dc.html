Which header where

A on the five tab roots. B on any pushed list or settings screen, and as what A and C collapse into. C only where a photo owns the top — place detail, review detail, profile of another member. D on anything presented modally.

Scroll behaviour

Large title fades and the compact bar crossfades in across 0→120px of scroll, driven off one shared value. The bar never hides on scroll-down — the tab bar never hides at all. Blur applies only once the compact bar is opaque enough to read.

Rules

One chartreuse element per bar — the pour button, or the active tab on ink, never both. Orange is badges only. Header circle buttons are 40px visual, 44px tap. Tapping the active tab scrolls that stack to top; long-press on the pour button opens the quick-log sheet.
