Question 2 · the real blocker

Stop competing for width. Stack the header.

Three columns — meters, overall, regulars — share 402pt on the bar page and far less in the map sheet, so “Presentation” truncates to “Pre…”. This is a layout problem, so the answer is a layout: the overall score and the two meters occupy one full-width row each, and the regulars become a horizontal avatar rail underneath. Nothing has to shrink, and the same block works at any sheet height.

New · contrast bug

The selected segment is invisible.

On Discover, the selected pill takes the chartreuse fill but keeps a pale label, so “Profiles” sits at roughly 1.3:1 on its own background — the selected tab is the one you cannot read. Chartreuse is a fill that always carries green-700 ink (onSecondary, 5.9:1). The unselected half needs the same treatment in reverse: green ink on paper, not a ghost.

Review item 2 · dev tool shipping

The rank preview row leaves the profile.

Live / Well / Call / Premium / Top with an “Actual” toggle is a ring-colour checker, and it sits between the identity block and the tabs where a real feature would go. Review item 3 · always half-hidden

Feed photo goes to 4:3 so the actions clear the fold.

At 4:5 the card is taller than a 6.1″ viewport, so like / comment / share is sliced by the tab bar on first paint — the primary engagement affordance is the one thing you never see. 4:3 gives back about 78pt, which is exactly the footer plus its padding.

Review item 4 · one value, two colours

Meter fill follows the surface, not the screen.

The bar page draws taste and presentation in white on green; the map sheet draws the same two numbers in pimento on white. One rule instead: ratingFill is chartreuse on green surfaces and pimento-500 on paper. The meter then always reads as the rating accent, wherever it appears.

Review item 5 · ~2:1 on bright photos

Overlay copy moves onto a solid plate.

Venue name, spirit and both meters currently float on the photo under a gradient — fine on a dark pour, near 2:1 on a bright one. Stop gambling on the image: the bottom block sits on a solid scrimStrong panel with the photo above it. Contrast becomes a constant instead of a property of the picture.

Question 1

A venue with no photo gets a typographic hero.

locations has no image column, and borrowing a member’s review photo makes the venue look like it endorsed one person’s Tuesday. Use the brand instead: a deep-green block, the name set lowercase in the display cut, the sticker badge pinned at a tilt. It is cheaper than venue photography and more on-brand than a stock bar shot. If a venue photo ever lands, the same block takes it as a background with the existing scrim — no relayout.

Question 3

Title-case venue names on the way in, not on the way out.

The Places API shouts. Normalise once at the service boundary rather than fighting it per screen: if a name is more than 80% uppercase, title-case it, keeping words of 2–3 letters that were already all-caps intact so acronyms survive. Store the normalised name; keep the raw string for search matching.

From the API COPPER CHIMNEY INDIAN RESTAURANT ONE65 SF O’ BY CLAUDE LE TOHIC
Normalised Copper Chimney Indian Restaurant ONE65 SF O’ by Claude Le Tohic

“ONE65 SF” survives because both tokens are short and already uppercase; the lowercase joiners in the third come from a small stop-word list (by, of, the, at, on, and, le, la, de).

Question 4

Disabled means the disabled surface, everywhere.

A dimmed green fill still reads as a live primary button — people tap it. Use disabledSurface + disabledText, the same pair the rest of the app uses, and put the reason under it so the state explains itself.
