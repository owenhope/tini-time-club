Map style

Paper land #F4F1EA, parks #E2EDE1, water #D7E6E3, roads pure white. No POI icons, no third-party labels — street and park names only, in DM Mono uppercase 9px at #8A938F. The map must never out-shout the pins.

Tap behaviour

Pin grows 38→52 over 180ms with a spring, siblings fade to 55%, the map pans so the pin lands at 38% height, the list sheet collapses and the peek card springs up in 240ms. Tap the map or swipe the card down to deselect. Tapping the card body pushes place detail.

Rules

Olives, never stars. Orange is reserved for the user dot — nothing else on the map may use it. Every pin's tap area is 44px even at 30px visual size. Clusters carry a count, never a score. Peek card actions are ≥46px.
