# Tini Time Club

Tini Time Club is a social martini reference and review app. Its language separates the reference collection from members’ individual reviews and bar orders.

## Language

**Martini Index**:
The curated reference collection of martini combinations members can browse and filter.
_Avoid_: Menu, catalog

**Martini Index Entry**:
One named martini combination in the Martini Index, including its spirit, style, ingredients, description, image, and suggested order.
_Avoid_: Menu item, drink listing

**Guide Note**:
A compact reference note inside the Martini Index that explains ordering language such as dry, wet, up, shaken, or stirred.
_Avoid_: Did-you-know card

**Up**:
A serving style where a drink is chilled with ice, then strained into a stemmed glass without ice.
_Avoid_: Neat

**Neat**:
A serving style where a spirit is poured without chilling, ice, or added dilution.
_Avoid_: Up

**Twist**:
A strip of citrus peel expressed over a drink to add aromatic oils and brightness.

**Olive garnish**:
An olive added to a martini for a savory, salty finish.

**Pick One**:
The preference-aware tool that selects a Martini Index Entry for a member after excluding spirits or styles they dislike.
_Avoid_: Order for me, randomizer
